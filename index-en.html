Why Çeyizim

Not a checklist. A coordination layer.

The real question isn't “what do we need”, it's “who's getting what, by when”. Çeyizim is the one place where the bride, groom and both families see the same list.

Family roles and sharing

Owner, partner, family member or guest. Share the 6-character invite code via WhatsApp; the other person joins from the “Enter Invite Code” screen. The free plan shares a list with up to 2 people; Premium removes the cap.

Who bought what, at a glance

Mark items “I got it”, assign them (“mom is getting this”) and follow the live activity feed. Snap a photo at the store and add it to the list.

See your budget, prepare calmly

Track the total budget, what's spent and what's left, category by category. Start in minutes with 9 categories and 160 ready-made items.

Start without an account

Mark a few items, feel the value, then sign up. Sign in with Apple or Google whenever you want backup or family sharing.
