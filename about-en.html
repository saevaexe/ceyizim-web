About

The official site of the Çeyizim: Çeyiz Listesi app.

What is Çeyizim?

Çeyizim is an iPhone and Android app for keeping a shared wedding and home checklist (a Turkish “çeyiz” list) with your partner and family. It starts with 160 suggested items across 9 categories. You assign who will buy each item, mark it as not bought or bought, and can enter prices to track your budget by category. The app has no ads.

The first iOS version was released in May 2026. The app is available in nine languages.

Who makes it?

Çeyizim is developed by Osman Seven. The app is published on the App Store and Google Play under the name Osman Seven.
