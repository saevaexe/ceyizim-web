Account & Data Deletion

Çeyizim (developer: Osman Seven) · Last updated: May 23, 2026

You can delete your Çeyizim account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways: from within the app, or by sending a request via email.

Option 1 — From within the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Çeyizim app.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm on the confirmation screen.

If you only want to sign out, choose "Log Out" instead — your data is kept in that case. "Delete Account" cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you cannot access the app, email us to delete your account: osman.seven97@icloud.com. Send the request from the email address associated with your account. After verifying your identity, we will delete your account and data.

Data that is deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data is permanently deleted:

  • Your account and sign-in details (name, email address)
  • Your trousseau lists and all items in them
  • Item photos you uploaded
  • Your budget data and notes
  • Your activity feed records
  • Your family memberships and invite links

Data that is kept and retention periods

  • Deletion timeline: Your data is permanently deleted from our servers (Firebase) within 30 days of confirmation.
  • Purchase / subscription records: Your subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store, Google Play and RevenueCat. Due to legal and accounting obligations, these transaction records may be retained by those platforms, outside our control, for a period of time. To cancel an active subscription, use your device's subscription settings.
  • Anonymous diagnostics: Aggregated, non-identifiable crash and usage statistics may remain in anonymous form after deletion.

For details about our privacy practices, see our Privacy Policy. If you run into any issues, reach us at osman.seven97@icloud.com.
